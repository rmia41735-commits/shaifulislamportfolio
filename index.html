Professional Profile

Shaiful Islam

My name is Shaiful Islam, and I work as a Weighbridge Scaler at Al Qaryan Group. I am involved in daily weighbridge operations, ensuring accurate vehicle weighing, material movement recording, transaction processing, and operational documentation.

My responsibilities include operating the WBMS (Weighbridge Management System), managing gross and tare weight records, verifying vehicle and material details, and maintaining accurate transaction data. I also work with SAP for Material Operations & Control, supporting inventory monitoring, material tracking, goods movement, and operational reporting.

Additionally, I use Microsoft Excel for data management and reporting, and Microsoft Outlook for professional business communication and correspondence.

I focus on accuracy, attention to detail, record integrity, operational efficiency, and responsible handling of business information to support smooth and reliable daily operations.
